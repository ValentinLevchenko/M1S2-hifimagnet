# M1S2-hifimagnet (Version 0.0.1)
This repository is dedicated to the S2 project of the CSMI Master's programme at the University of Strasbourg, in collaboration with Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS) and Laboratoire National des Champs Magnétiques Intenses (LNCMI)

## Objective 
Statistical characterisation and exploratory data analysis (EDA) of magnet operational data, based on experimental records, in order to: 
- identify patterns;
- aggregate lifetime statistics;
- establish relationships between physical models and various operating conditions.
